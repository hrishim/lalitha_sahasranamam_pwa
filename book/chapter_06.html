Nāma 501

गुडान्नप्रीतमानसा

Samāsa-vigraha

गुडेन मिश्रमन्नं तेन प्रीतं मानसं यस्याः सा।

Word by word

गुडेन — with jaggery.

मिश्रमन्नं — rice/food mixed with it.

तेन — by that.

प्रीतं — pleased.

मानसं — mind.

यस्याः — whose.

सा — she.

गुडान्नप्रीतमानसा — the complete name, understood through the samasa above.

Meaning

(i) One who gets satisfied with rice cooked with jaggery.

Esoteric note

Bhāskararāya traces the exact dietary preferences that conclude the Maṇipūra sequence:

The Alchemical Sweetness: Jaggery (guḍa) represents the earthy, unctuous sweetness required to cool and safely ground the violent flames of the digestive and energetic fire (Agni) located at the navel center, ensuring that the intense kinetic heat does not exhaust the physical organs.
