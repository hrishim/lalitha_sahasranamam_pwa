Nāma 801

पुष्टा

Samāsa-vigraha

षट्त्रिंशत्तत्त्वविग्रहशीलत्वात्।

ब्राह्मणैर्वा — component from the samasa above

पुष्टा। — component from the samasa above

Meaning

(i) One who is full and nourished.

(ii) She is complete with the thirty-six tattvas and with countless qualities.

(iii) She nourishes the universe and is Herself the fullness from which nourishment comes.

Esoteric note

In the Saubhāgyabhāskara, Bhāskararāya details the metaphysical expansion behind this fullness:

The Fullness of the Thirty-Six Tattvas: She is Puṣṭā because Her manifest body is perfectly filled and nourished by the complete deployment of the thirty-six foundational principles of reality (षट्त्रिंशत्तत्त्व).

The Essence of Brahman: Alternatively, She is continually nourished and amplified by absolute virtues, the nectar of Brahman-bliss (brahma-rasa), and the spiritual realizations of realized seers, remaining absolute without decay.
