Word by word

ह्रींकारी — meaning follows from the samasa: ह्रीं करोतीति।

Meaning

i. One who is in the form of Hrim bija letters.

ii. Hri means modesty, shyness and bashfulness. One who does this is Hrimkari. In Saptashati it is mentioned that we bow to Sreedevee, who is in the form of bashfulness in all souls.

iii. In Swatantra Tantra it is mentioned that since Sreedevee takes care of creation, protection and destruction tasks, She is called Hrimkari.

iv. Hrim itself is one of the names of Sreedevee, the 99th name in Lalita Trishati also. Sree Adi Sankara mentions that all energies are attributes of Sreedevee; by disconnecting them, we get Her liberated form.

v. This is the maya-bija, also called Bhuvaneshvari bija.

vi. This bija is in the Panchadashi mantra at the end of every division. The letters Ka and Ha correspond to Shiva, and the letters A, E, La and Sa correspond to Shakti.

vii. Om in general is called Pranavam. This is also called Shakta Pranavam.

viii. Lalita Trishati has three hundred names, twenty for each letter of the Panchadashi mantra; sixty names correspond to Hrim. The detailed meanings can be seen in the commentary of Sree Adi Sankara.

Esoteric note

In the Saubhāgyabhāskara, Bhāskararāya unpacks the immense ontological weight of this bīja within Śrīvidyā theology:

The Cosmic Sound Form: ह्रीं is the supreme Lajjā-bīja (syllable of cosmic modesty/containment). Bhāskararāya details that She is both the source and the ultimate reality indicated by this syllable. It represents the unmanifested consciousness containing the seeds of the entire universe, ready to project outward.

The Power of Manifestation: As Hrīṁkārī, She animates the primal vibration that marks the boundary line between the absolute unmanifested Brahman (Nirguṇa) and the manifested, qualified universe (Saguṇa).

Esoteric note

Bhāskararāya applies the Vedāntic concept of the uninvolved witness (Sākṣī):

The Cosmic Witness: The universe moves through cycles of creation, maintenance, and collapse. She stands as the constant, unmoving lens that witnesses all of these transitions simultaneously. She does not get entangled in the changes; She is the light that makes the perception of the universe possible.

Nāma 385

साक्षिवर्जिता

साक्षिवर्जिता — beyond any witness

Meaning

i. One who has no witness beyond herself.

ii. She is the witness of all, but there is no other witness to her. Since she is the ultimate consciousness, she is beyond being witnessed by another.

Esoteric note

Bhāskararāya constructs another profound non-dual paradox by matching 384 and 385:

The Absolute Subject: While She witnesses everything (Viśvasākṣiṇī), there is absolutely no one else back behind Her to witness Her. She is the ultimate, final subjective consciousness (Draṣṭā). There is no second entity above or beyond Her to turn Her into an object, confirming Her status as the final absolute.

The Six Angas: In mantra practice, a text is consecrated across six limbs: heart (Hṛdaya), head (Śiras), crown-lock (Śikhā), armor (Kavaca), eyes (Netra), and weapon (Astra). She is Ṣaḍaṅgadevatāyuktā because Her supreme presence is perfectly protected and expressed through these six secondary guardian forces.

Perpetual Compassion: To be "moist" means Her heart never dries up into cold indifference. She is perpetually soft and melting with mercy for Her children.

The Nityā Goddess: It also explicitly names Her identity as नित्यक्लिन्ना, the third Nityā goddess governing the structural flow of lunar time and nectar within the Śrī Cakra.

Beyond Comparison: A comparison can only occur if there are two similar things in the universe. Since She is the unique, non-dual, unmatched absolute reality (Advaya), there is absolutely nothing else in existence that can be placed next to Her as an equal or an analogy.
